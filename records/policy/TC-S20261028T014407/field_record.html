Policy (Toceta, toceta-policy-section/0.1)

The session ran V1's own hand rule (any hand in the box): no distance threshold to compare.

Frame-to-hold here is t_hold_packet - t_frame_pump, a lower bound of the public t_hold_packet - t_capture: no camera recorder ran, so no component logged the capture time.

V1's hand monitor reads its frames from the runner's frame pump, so today the watchdog's pixels are supplied by the policy host; the hand detector runs separately, but the hold decision, the chain and the policy run in one process, the runner; Part 3 moves the camera, the decision and the record to the watchdog's own computer.

Each S says how far a demonstration threshold falls short under that geometry; neither is a safety claim, and d (and H unless set) is assumed, not measured.

The record locates; it does not diagnose. A worn gripper and a bad policy look the same in a window.
